# Dashboard: Margins & Profitability

## Objective
Analyze gross margin and profitability by product, category, and channel to optimize business performance.

---

## Metrics & Visualizations

- **Gross margin by product, category, channel**
  - Visualization: Bar chart, KPI card
  - Source: fact_sales, dim_product, dim_channel

- **Top products/channels by profitability**
  - Visualization: Horizontal bar chart, Table
  - Source: fact_sales, dim_product, dim_channel

---

## Recommended Filters
- Period (day, week, month, year)
- Product category
- Channel

---

## Tables used
- fact_sales
- dim_product
- dim_channel
