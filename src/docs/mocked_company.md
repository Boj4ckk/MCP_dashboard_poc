## Business Overview
Patissier is a large-scale retail company operating in the electronics and multimedia sector. The company distributes consumer electronics products such as televisions, audio systems, household appliances, computers, and connected devices.

It operates through multiple sales channels including physical stores across France and a large e-commerce platform.

---

## Core Business Model
- B2C retail distribution
- Omnichannel sales (online + physical stores)
- High-volume product catalog with frequent turnover
- Strong dependency on logistics and inventory optimization

---

## Product Categories
- Television & Displays (LED, OLED, Smart TVs)
- Audio & Home Entertainment (speakers, headphones, sound systems)
- Home Appliances (washing machines, refrigerators, microwaves)
- Computing (laptops, desktops, accessories)
- Mobile & Connected Devices (smartphones, wearables, IoT devices)

---

## Sales Channels
- E-commerce platform (primary growth driver)
- Physical retail stores across France
- Third-party marketplaces (selective partnerships)

---

## Operational Footprint
- Multiple warehouses distributed across France
- Regional store network covering major cities
- Centralized supply chain management

---

## Key Business Objectives
- Maximize revenue across channels
- Optimize product margins
- Reduce stockouts and overstock situations
- Improve customer retention and lifetime value
- Enhance after-sales service efficiency

---

## Main Operational Challenges
- High inventory complexity due to large product catalog
- Supply chain variability affecting stock availability
- Strong competition in electronics retail market
- Price sensitivity of customers
- Product return and after-sales service management
